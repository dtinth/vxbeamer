Add pairing to the settings sheet: paste the link the backend's `/pair` page shows, or scan its QR code with the qr.spacet.me scanner, to sign in without running the OIDC flow in this browser. Useful where the sign-in cannot complete here, such as an iOS home-screen app, where Google shows you as logged out.

The link names the backend, so pairing also sets the backend URL.
